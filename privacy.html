Privacy Policy

Last updated 7 October 2026. APEX XI is a football game. It does not show ads, does not track you across other apps or websites, and does not sell or share your data with anyone.

Playing offline

Your clubs, cards, careers, settings and kept goals are saved on your device only. Nothing about offline play is sent to us.

If you create an online account

An account is optional and only needed for online matches, guilds, friends and cloud saves. We store:

  • the player name you choose and a one-way hash of your password (never the password itself);
  • a copy of your save, so it can follow you to another device;
  • your online record: matches, ratings, your friends list and your guild;
  • sign-in tokens, so you stay signed in on your devices.

We do not ask for your email address, real name, location, contacts or photos. Players can only talk to each other with preset phrases and emotes, and there is no free-text chat.

Crash reports

If the game hits an error, it may send a short report: the game version, the error message and where in the game it happened. The report contains no account name or personal information, and it is used only to fix bugs.

Network data

Like any server, ours sees the IP address of each connection. We use it only to limit abuse, such as repeated wrong passwords, and we do not store it with your account.

Deleting your data

You can delete your online account at any time in the game: Online → Delete account. This removes your name, password hash, cloud save, online record, friends and guild seat from our server straight away. Progress saved on your device stays until you delete the game or use Settings → Reset.

Children

APEX XI does not knowingly collect personal information from children, and an account needs nothing more than a made-up name and a password.

Contact

Questions about this policy or your data: support@apexxi.online.
