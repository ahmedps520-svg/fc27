Support

Something not working, or an idea for the game? Email support@apexxi.online and we will get back to you.

Contact

Email support@apexxi.online. If you are reporting a bug, include the Version and Build from Settings → App (on iPhone also the App build), and what you were doing when it happened.

Do I need an account?

No. Every mode except online play works offline, and your progress is saved on your device. A free account, which needs only a player name and a password, lets you play online and keeps a copy of your save in the cloud.

Playing online

Open the Online tab, create an account or sign in, then find an opponent or share a lobby code with a friend. The iPhone app and the website play each other. Players can only send preset phrases and emotes; there is no open chat.

Purchases

On iPhone, packs are opened with coins you earn by playing. There are no real-money purchases in the iPhone app.

Deleting your account

In the game: Online → Delete account. Your online account, cloud save, online record, friends and guild seat are removed straight away. Progress saved on your device stays until you delete the app or use Settings → Reset. See the privacy policy for what is stored.

Controls

On a phone, hold it sideways: the left side of the screen is the stick and the buttons sit under your right thumb. Game controllers work too. Every control can be changed in Settings → Controls.
