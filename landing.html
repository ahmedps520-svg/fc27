Pick how you play

Every mode runs on the same match engine, so the football is the same everywhere. Only what's riding on it changes.

Kick Off

Straight into a match

Any two clubs, any ground, any weather, day or night. Play alone, with a friend on your side, or against them on the same screen.

Ultimate XI

Build a club from cards

Open packs, fit the squad together for chemistry, and climb eleven divisions. Squad challenges, evolutions and weekly events keep it moving.

Career

Manager or player

Run a club through seasons of transfers, budgets and board meetings. Or create a footballer and play only your own position.

Street

Three a side, five on the sand

Small pitches, quick games, skill moves that earn style. Make a baller and take over the courts.

Online

Against friends, or with them

Head-to-head matches, co-op seasons, and a spectator seat. Chat is quick phrases and emotes only.

Skills

Practise

Skill games for dribbling, passing, crossing and free kicks, a penalty shootout, and a pitch to yourself. Your best scores are kept.
